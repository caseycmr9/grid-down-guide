# Fact-check log: disasters, power, shelter, security
Checked 2026-11-16 by AI against the sources listed. NOT a substitute for review by a qualified person.

Files: `data/disasters.js` (15 articles), `data/power.js` (6), `data/shelter.js` (9), `data/security.js` (6). All four pass `node tools/validate.mjs`.

**Method and limits.** Primary pages were opened with WebFetch where the site allowed it. Several official pages returned 403/404 (FSIS, NHC, USGS, EPA, NHTSA, CDC clinical pages), and the shared web-search budget ran out partway through, so some claims could not be re-checked. Those are logged **Unverified** and left in place only where the wording is already hedged or the risk is low. "(search-result summary)" means I saw the fact only in a search-tool summary of the named page, not on the page itself. The most useful single source was the NFPA Educational Messages Desk Reference, 2026 edition (cited as "NFPA EMDR" with its section numbers).
