## Needs human expert review
- **Infant CPR hand technique and infant choking chest thrusts** were changed to the 2025 AHA wording (heel of one hand / two-thumb encircling). Please confirm against AHA 2025 Part 6 primary text; heart.org blocked the checker.
- **Open (sucking) chest wound:** sources genuinely disagree (TCCC/military: vented or three-sided seal; AHA/ARC and ERC: lay first aiders should leave open or use a non-occlusive dressing). The article now presents both. An EMT should choose the final wording.
- **Coral snake pressure immobilization:** article now says trained persons only, when care is far away (Red Cross wording). 2024 AHA/ARC table reportedly calls pressure immobilization "potentially harmful" for snakebites; confirm.
- **Packing pressure time:** 10 min for plain gauze is from NAEMT B-Con; some Stop the Bleed courses teach 3 min for any packing. Conservative value used.
- **Anaphylaxis second-dose window:** article keeps 5-15 min (AAAAI/ACAAI style); Red Cross says 5-10 min; nasal epinephrine label says 5 min. Not contradictory but worth harmonizing.
- **Epinephrine under 15 kg:** article says use prescribed device, else the larger one in a true emergency. Confirm with pediatric allergy guidance.
- **Heat stroke stop-cooling threshold** 101-102 °F vs WMS-cited 38.3-38.8 °C: close; confirm.
- **Downed power line distance** (35 ft) and **post-drowning observation** (4-8 h): unverified exact figures.
- **Crisis script "seizure"** links to `chronic-conditions` (health.js, not checked here). Its steps follow CDC seizure first aid (time it, protect head, nothing in mouth, side position, call 911 if over 5 min); the "loosen neck clothing" and "first seizure / in water" call triggers are CDC items not in the health.js text.
- **Crisis script "low-blood-sugar"** follows health.js (15 g, recheck in 15 min, glucagon).
