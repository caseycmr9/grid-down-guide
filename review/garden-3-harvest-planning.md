## Needs human expert review
- **Greywater law is the most delicate item.** The current California Plumbing Code (2025, Chapter 15) and Health and Safety Code 17922.12 were not read directly. The text above rests on city handouts that quote the 2016 and 2025 code, plus the 2007 Chapter 16A text. A plumbing inspector or county environmental health office should confirm: the laundry-to-landscape conditions, that other systems need a permit, and the 24-hour storage limit. Notification to the local agency may be required in some places. Some sources mention "dark graywater" (kitchen sink and dishwasher water) as a newer term in some local codes; the article keeps the conservative line that kitchen sink and dishwasher water is not greywater.
- **Rainwater.** Only the state water-right permit exemption was verified (Water Code 10574). Local building and plumbing rules, size limits, and drinking-water use were not researched. The article tells readers to check local rules and treat water before drinking.
- **Wildfire soil.** UC gives no pass/fail lead or arsenic number and says risk is judged case by case. The article therefore gives no numbers and sends readers to a lab. Arsenic (for example from burned treated wood) is not named in the UC documents and is not named in the article. A Purdue summary seen in search results answers "No" to whether produce is safe after a chemical fire, which is stricter than UC's smoke-only wording; the article follows UC and keeps the "discard if coated" rule.
- **Shade cloth 30-50%,** low-desert summer crops (Arizona sources, not Imperial or Coachella), and "foothills" soil timing rest on search-result summaries.
- **Drought rules** change often. The State Water Board page seen in search results said the statewide emergency conservation regulations expired June 5, 2024, while a 2023 FAQ called them in effect. The article tells readers to check their water supplier.
- **Cover-crop wait of 3-6 weeks** comes from a UC Master Gardener summary seen in search results; the page returned 403. A UC Master Gardener should confirm, and whether bell bean residue needs the longer end (one result said 5-6 weeks).
