## Summary
- Claims checked: 125
- Confirmed: 91
- Corrected: 15
- Softened: 12
- Unverified: 7

Main findings:
- The crop tables (distance, plants, life cycle, pollination) match the current Seed Savers Exchange (SSE) seed saving chart as printed by Community Seed Network and Univ. of Illinois Extension. Distances in the article are at the cautious end of the published range, as the article says.
- Corn isolation: sources really do disagree (1/2 mile in SSE's current chart, 1 mile in Illinois text, 2 miles in New Mexico State and WSU Skagit master gardener). The article's "1-2 miles" is kept and the disagreement is now stated plainly. See the note at the end about `data/garden.js`.
- Fixed: seed life for the Brassica rapa row; tomato fermentation temperature (no source gives one); weevil freezing time; potato chitting time; sweet potato slip timing and removal method; garlic planting depth; rhubarb division interval; grape cutting length; scion storage temperature; the cucurbitacin warning (added stress cause, symptoms, when to get help); treated-seed warning; plastic and paper bagging advice.

## Needs human expert review
- **Corn isolation distance.** SSE's current chart lists 800 ft to 1/2 mile; Illinois text says "a mile"; New Mexico State and WSU Skagit master gardeners say 2 miles. The article keeps 1-2 miles and says sources differ. A seed-saving expert should confirm the wording.
- **Squash species crossing.** Iowa State and UNL Lancaster say only the same species cross; SDSU groups butternut with maxima types; NC State's cucurbit breeding page lists the species as intercrossable by hand. The article says crosses are rare and sources disagree. Please confirm, and confirm the cushaw (argyrosperma vs moschata) statement.
- **Seed life.** Extension tables disagree by 1-3 years for many crops (for example tomato 3 yr at UNL, 4 at USU, 5 at older SSE; pepper 2 yr at UNL, 4 at USU). The article gives ranges and calls them guidelines.
- **Plant counts.** The article uses SSE's "variety maintenance" range. The older SSE chart used larger single numbers (peas 25, carrots 60, onions 50, spinach 50).
- **Weevil freezing.** NC State says 4 days at 0 F; Utah State and OSU say 24-30 hours; Penn State is reported (search result) at 48 hours. The article says at least 4 days.
- **Fermentation temperature.** No extension source gives a temperature, only "room temperature". The article gives a room-temperature range (68-77 F) that is a definition of room temperature, not a source figure.
- **Desiccants.** Amounts and the 1-2 week finishing time are not published in any source I opened.
- **Slip removal.** Missouri says cut 1-2 in above the soil line; Clemson, Maryland and Illinois say pull or twist. Slip timing ranges from 6 weeks (Maryland, Illinois) to 8-9 weeks (Missouri).
- **Garlic planting windows** vary by region (California coast vs interior vs desert, and 2-8 weeks before freeze in cold climates). Local Extension should confirm.
- **Seed-potato curing, storage and planting numbers** were not independently sourced here (they match `growing-potatoes-roots`). Certified-seed rules vary by state.
- **Grafting and cutting lengths** vary by species and source (grape 12-18 in, fig 6-12 in). A local fruit grower or Extension horticulturist should confirm for the target climate.
- **Legal statements** (PVP, utility and plant patents, OSSI, state seed-swap rules) are kept general. A seed-law specialist could confirm; the article says it is not legal advice.
- **Unverified items:** sunflower wild-crossing and seed life, basil isolation, true potato seed behaviour, horseradish, Jerusalem artichoke and walking onion notes, herb cuttings and division.
